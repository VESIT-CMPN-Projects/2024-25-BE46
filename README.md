Tomato plants are highly susceptible to a variety of diseases that can significantly reduce crop yield and quality. Early detection of these diseases is essential for effective management and reduction of economic losses in agriculture. This project proposes a deep learning-based approach to identify and classify tomato plant diseases using image recognition techniques. A convolutional neural network (CNN) model is trained on a dataset of tomato leaf images, including healthy leaves and those affected by common diseases such as early blight, late blight, and leaf mold.

The model utilizes TensorFlow Lite to ensure lightweight deployment on mobile and embedded devices, enabling farmers to scan plant leaves using a smartphone camera and receive instant disease diagnosis. The system enhances agricultural productivity by providing a low-cost, accessible, and efficient tool for disease prediction and management. The integration of AI in plant disease detection contributes to the advancement of smart farming and supports sustainable agriculture practices.
